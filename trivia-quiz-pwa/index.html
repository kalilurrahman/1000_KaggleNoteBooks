ℹ️ How to play

🎯 Classic Quiz

Pick a topic, difficulty and quiz length. Answer multiple-choice questions at your own pace. You get two 50:50 lifelines that remove two wrong answers, and streaks multiply your score (+10 per correct, +2 per streak level).

🟦 Jeopardy

A board of 5 categories × 5 values ($200–$1000). Pick any clue; a correct answer adds its value, a wrong one subtracts it. One hidden Daily Double doubles the stakes. Each clue has a 20-second timer. Clear the board or cash out anytime.

🧠 Mastermind

Inspired by the BBC classic: Round 1 is 90 seconds on your chosen specialist subject; Round 2 is 120 seconds of general knowledge. Answer as many as you can — passing is allowed but the clock never stops. 10 points per correct answer.

⚡ Rapid Fire

Tata Crucible-style buzzer round: one 90-second blitz across all topics. +10 for a correct answer, −5 for a wrong one, and every 3-in-a-row streak awards a +15 bonus. Passing is free — but time is not.

🌐 Online mode

With an internet connection, Classic mode can pull fresh questions from the Open Trivia Database — 24 categories and thousands of questions. Offline, the built-in bank of 330+ questions keeps every mode playable.

📱 Install as an app

QuizVerse is a PWA: use your browser's Install / Add to Home Screen option to play it like a native app, fully offline.
